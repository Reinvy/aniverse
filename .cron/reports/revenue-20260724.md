## Recommendations

1. **🚀 Launch Stripe Integration:** Top priority. Payment processing is the
   single blocker for all revenue streams.

2. **📊 Set Up Analytics Tracking:** Implement PostHog/Mixpanel to track:
   - Conversion funnel from signup → paid
   - Feature usage by plan tier
   - Retention cohorts

3. **💰 Introduce Coin System Early:** Even before full subscription launch,
   coin purchases can generate early revenue and validate willingness to pay.

4. **🎯 Target Free → Pro Upgrade Prompts:** In-app upsell after users hit
   5+ generations — highest intent moment.

5. **📧 Email Drip Campaign for Warm Leads:** Users who have been active
   for 7+ days should receive the premium upgrade email sequence (see
   `src/data/monetization/promo-20260724.ts`).

6. **🤝 Activate Referral Program at Launch:** Word-of-mouth will be the
   primary growth channel — make it easy to share.
