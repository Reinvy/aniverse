## Recommendations

1. **🚀 Launch Stripe Integration (Critical Path):** Payment processing remains the
   single blocker for all revenue streams. This should be the #1 priority for A2
   Product Development.

2. **💰 Enable Coin Purchases First:** Even before full subscription launch, coin
   purchases can generate early revenue and validate willingness to pay. This
   requires only Stripe Checkout — simpler than full subscription management.

3. **🎯 In-App Upgrade Prompts:** Add upgrade CTA after users hit 5+ generations
   in a session — highest intent moment. Target the 13% power users first.

4. **📧 Email Drip Campaign:** Users active for 7+ days should receive the premium
   upgrade email sequence (see `src/data/monetization/promo-20260725.ts`).

5. **📊 Analytics Setup:** Implement PostHog or similar to track:
   - Conversion funnel from signup → paid
   - Feature usage by plan tier
   - Retention cohorts
   - Referral program effectiveness

6. **🤝 Activate Referral Program at Launch:** Word-of-mouth is the primary growth
   channel. Make referral sharing frictionless from the dashboard.

7. **🏆 Challenge Monetization:** A10's challenges could include premium-entry
   contests with cash prizes — driving both engagement and revenue.
